04 / RF feasibility

Can the scanner reach frequencies between the 16 presets?

Yes, within the CC1101 operating bands, with an important qualification. The hardware can be programmed across its supported bands; the current application simply chooses 16 points. It cannot receive the entire 300-928 MHz span because the CC1101 has three separated operating bands.

hardware capability

Continuous tuning inside three bands

Official documentation lists receive coverage at 300-348 MHz, 387-464 MHz, and 779-928 MHz. TI lists a programmed frequency resolution of approximately 397-412 Hz for a 26-27 MHz crystal.

band-limited~400 Hz tuned step
current implementation

Preset-only quick survey

rf_channels[] has 16 entries. No user-selected RX frequency, span, step, dwell, progress, or refinement pass exists. The TX presets are a separate four-entry list and do not expand RX coverage.

Decision needed

Choose whether the next RF prototype should optimize for fast room survey, unknown-signal discovery, or frequency characterization. One screen cannot optimize all three without making the controls and scan time confusing.

05 / proposed interface contract

Signal meter as the primary UI

Your instinct is right for active sensing modes. The rule should be: show the strongest honest numeric signal first, then the evidence list. GPS and Info need health/status meters rather than pretending every tab has an RF-style dBm value.

interaction rule

Every meter needs freshness

Show live, last sample 2.4 s, or stale. A frozen number is worse than an honest empty state when the user is moving a device around a room.

interaction rule

Meter and list are not the same thing

The bar should answer “is anything getting stronger?” The list should answer “what is it?” Keep both, but do not make a scrolling transcript carry the burden of a meter.

08 / next conversation

Decisions that unlock the next part

safety contract

Should RF TX exist at all?

The mission currently says passive RX only. My recommendation is to remove or hard-disable TX before improving the scanner. If you want a controlled test transmitter, that is a separate product mode with a new safety and legal contract.

RF scope

Quick survey or discovery sweep?

Choose between keeping the 16-point view, adding coarse band sweeps plus peak refinement, or building a characterization tool. The best meter layout depends on this choice.

BFFB contract

Can we pin the response format?

The Wi-Fi/BLE meter needs a stable line or packet contract with RSSI, identity, and freshness. The current app cannot infer that reliably from arbitrary raw output.

feedback policy

Should sound default OFF or ON?

Current defaults are OFF, and ON is not wired. Once real feedback exists, decide the default and use an immediate test pulse when the setting changes so “enabled” is observable.

Recommended build order

1. Resolve passive RX versus TX. 2. Make sound/vibro controls truthful. 3. Add a BFFB handshake and response parser fixture. 4. Add Wi-Fi/BLE meters and freshness. 5. Fix GPS stale-state and short-GLL bounds. 6. Prototype coarse RF sweep plus peak refinement. 7. Re-run the full device matrix and update the guide from the resulting contract.
