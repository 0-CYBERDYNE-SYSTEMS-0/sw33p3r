GLOBAL INPUTS

Short ◀ / ▶Change tab. In Settings: change group.
Hold ◀Analyzer on/off (RF Wi BT nR). nR: Hold OK also toggles.
Hold ▶Next page in tab or analyzer. TX disarmed+EXT: ExtBand.
▲ / ▼Browse / RF sub-mode / GPS+Info pages. Settings: item.
Hold ▲ / ▼RF only: open lock card. Short ▲/▼ then returns to map.
OKPrimary action (scan, start/stop, arm, mark, toggle).
Hold OKLock target. TX armed: transmit. GPS: NMEA retry. nR: analyzer.
Back / Hold BackSettings or disarm TX. Hold Back exits.

RSSI is not distance. Beacon/ad is not telemetry or intent. No observation is not proof of absence. TX is a bounded carrier test only — no jam, deauth, or replay. nRF24 is RPD activity, not mousejack. Analyzer radar rings are RSSI. Real meters exist only on GPS Radar with a fix. ScanWin lives in Settings, not Hold ▶.
