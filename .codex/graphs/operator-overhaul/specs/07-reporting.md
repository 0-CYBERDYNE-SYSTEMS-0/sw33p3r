# Plain-English reporting

The report must answer, without specialist terminology where avoidable:

- Was the session completed cleanly?
- Which sensors and links were actually available and confirmed?
- What radio observations were strongest or repeated, and when?
- What scan modes and windows produced them?
- Was GPS fresh, and were coordinates deliberately omitted or included?
- Was a TX carrier merely armed, actually started, stopped, refused, or aborted?
- Were records dropped, truncated, capped, or stopped by a storage error?
- Which files belong to this run?

It must also say:

- RSSI is a relative stronger/weaker observation, not distance.
- Beacon/frame/advertisement evidence does not prove Internet telemetry,
  recording, ownership, or intent.
- No observation does not prove absence.
- The TX tool generates a bounded carrier at a frequency; it does not replay the
  observed device's original signal.
